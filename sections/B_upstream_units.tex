\section{Upstream unit parameters}
\label{app:units}

\Cref{tab:units} lists the parameters of the CHP plants in \cref{eq:chp-bp,eq:chp-ex,eq:chp-limits}. All plants have an availability factor of $\alpha_u=0.9$. The remaining upstream units are aggregated: gas, oil and biomass boilers with capacities of 1,032, 1,040 and 41~MW, efficiencies of 0.92, 0.90 and 0.90 and fuel prices of 350, 600 and 200~DKK/MWh; electric boilers with 200~MW and an efficiency of 0.99; a common peak-boiler capacity of $\overline Q^{\mathrm{pb}}=2{,}033$~MW; and regional heat pumps with 20~MW and a COP of 3.2. The variable operating cost is 5~DKK/MWh of heat for boilers and 10~DKK/MWh for waste incineration.
